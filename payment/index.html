Frequently Asked Questions

Is this internship accepted for VTU and university academic credits?
Yes. OmniIDE is an AICTE Approved Technical Education Partner and MSME registered entity. The 3-month curriculum complies with university mandatory internship guidelines (including 7th & 8th semester VTU engineering credits). You receive an official verifiable certificate with a unique QR code and project completion evaluation report.
What is the expected weekly time commitment?
The program requires approximately 10–15 hours per week. Sprints are asynchronous with scheduled weekly mentor syncs, allowing you to balance regular semester exams and classes without conflict.
How is 1-on-1 mentor guidance structured?
Upon enrollment, a senior software engineer from our core engineering team is assigned to you based on your selected track. They conduct weekly 1-on-1 sprint reviews, unblock technical issues, audit your pull requests, and provide guidance on system architecture.
When will I receive my portal access after payment?
Immediately after registration, an on-screen reference ID is generated. Our engineering admissions team will dispatch your official Internship Portal username, password, onboarding documentation, and mentor calendar link to your registered email and WhatsApp within 2–4 hours.
